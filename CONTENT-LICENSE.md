## What counts as content

- **Art:** every image, sprite sheet, portrait, background, icon, cover,
  splash and logo, wherever it sits in the repository. That includes
  `packages/asset-contract/assets/`, `_CommericalArt/`,
  `apps/client/public/icons/` and the screenshots and reference images under
  `docs/` and `tests/`.
- **Music and sound:** every song, preview, announcer line, sound effect and
  ambience track (`.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav` and the like), wherever it
  sits.
- **Video:** every video file in the repository.
- **Story and characters:** the story, dialogue and character text, the
  characters' names, looks and descriptions, the song titles, and the stage
  names and descriptions (for example `content/characters/`, `content/songs/`,
  `content/stages/` and the story and tutorial text).

  and their logos. See `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## How the content was made

The songs were made by SNEKPRTY's owner on a paid Suno plan. Most of the art
was made with OpenAI's image tools; the covers, icon and splash were supplied
by the owner, and some cues and placeholders were drawn in this repository.
The sound effects were made on ElevenLabs under a plan that allows commercial
use. Suno's paid plan and OpenAI's terms assign to the user whatever rights
exist in what they make, and SNEKPRTY reserves all of those rights. Every art
and sound file's source is recorded in
`packages/asset-contract/assets/art-manifest.json`.
